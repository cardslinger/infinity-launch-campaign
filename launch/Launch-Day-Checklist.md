# Infinity Launch-Day Checklist

## Current gate (checked 2026-10-08)

- Infinity Zero is the current native desktop prototype. Its installed C# gateway completed a local Dolphin chat request; the on-screen composer and cross-app features still require demonstration and verification before being described as working.
- Infinity and Infinity² are roadmap tiers. Hardware, medical, and satellite concepts must not be presented as shipping rewards without engineering, cost, regulatory, and fulfillment evidence.
- The campaign preview site and press preview are public. A Kickstarter project, social launch posts, and paid ads are **not** verified live. No ad spending is authorized by this checklist.
- Before pledge collection: complete creator identity and payment onboarding, approve final campaign assets, cost every reward, set realistic delivery dates, and supply a live Kickstarter project URL. Do not substitute the Kickstarter homepage for a project link.

## Kickstarter rules review (checked 2026-10-06)

Official rules: https://www.kickstarter.com/rules

- Demonstrate the actual software prototype. Do not use rendered concepts to imply working functionality that does not exist; disclose undeveloped hardware/software dependencies.
- Explain how AI was used in the product and campaign materials, including generated concept art.
- Keep equity and contests/raffles out of reward tiers. Rewards must be new and designed or made by the project or collaborators; a third-party McLaren does not fit that rule as an ordinary reward unless a compliant, genuinely project-created offering is established and Kickstarter approves it. Do not promise one in the draft.
- Confirm creator identity, entity details, payment setup, reward costs, and delivery dates before submission.

- Replace the placeholder Kickstarter URL everywhere with the live campaign URL.
- Confirm campaign title, subtitle, hero image, video, funding goal, duration, and reward delivery dates.
- Verify all claims against the current build and roadmap.
- Remove any equity, ROI, loan, or financial-return language.
- Confirm every reward can actually be fulfilled at its stated cost and delivery date.
- Verify campaign visuals distinguish concepts, prototypes, and shipping products.
- Publish the campaign landing page.
- Update website hero and footer CTA to the Kickstarter URL.
- Publish X, Threads, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, TikTok, and YouTube launch posts.
- Publish launch video and pin the Kickstarter link.
- Send press release and press kit.
- Notify Discord/community channels.
- Pin the campaign link across owned profiles.
- Test the Kickstarter link on desktop and mobile.
- Track referral sources with campaign-specific UTM links where appropriate.
- Post first campaign update after launch with the current milestone and next target.
