# Infinity Launch-Day Checklist

- Replace the placeholder Kickstarter URL everywhere with the live campaign URL.
- Confirm campaign title, subtitle, hero image, video, funding goal, duration, and reward delivery dates.
- Verify all claims against the current build and roadmap.
- Remove any equity, ROI, loan, or financial-return language.
- Confirm every reward can actually be fulfilled at its stated cost and delivery date.
- Verify campaign visuals distinguish concepts, prototypes, and shipping products.
- Publish the campaign landing page.
- Update website hero and footer CTA to the Kickstarter URL.
- Publish X, Threads, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, TikTok, and YouTube launch posts.
- Publish launch video and pin the Kickstarter link.
- Send press release and press kit.
- Notify Discord/community channels.
- Pin the campaign link across owned profiles.
- Test the Kickstarter link on desktop and mobile.
- Track referral sources with campaign-specific UTM links where appropriate.
- Post first campaign update after launch with the current milestone and next target.
