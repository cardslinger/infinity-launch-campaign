## Infinity Launches Campaign for an Omniplatform AI Companion Built to Connect the Digital World

Infinity today announced its launch campaign for a new omniplatform AI companion designed to unify apps, devices, automations, preferences, AI services, and connected environments through one persistent user-controlled layer.

Built around the idea **“Reality. Your Way.”**, Infinity aims to reduce the fragmentation created by separate apps, devices, assistants, interfaces, and settings. Its architecture is designed around synchronized preferences, extensible integrations, automation, privacy controls, and a persistent AI companion that can operate across supported environments.

The campaign presents Infinity as more than a single application: a platform intended to evolve across software, mobile, desktop, connected devices, smart environments, and future hardware.

Supporters can follow and back the campaign on Kickstarter:
https://www.kickstarter.com/

### About Infinity
Infinity is an omniplatform AI companion and digital ecosystem built around one portable preference graph, cross-device continuity, extensible integrations, automation, privacy controls, and user-directed behavior.
