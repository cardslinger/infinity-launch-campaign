INFINITY

Reality. Your Way.

Your devices. Your world. Your Infinity.

Infinity is an omniplatform AI companion built to make your digital life work as one system instead of a pile of disconnected apps, devices, accounts, automations, and settings.

One account. One preference graph. One AI companion. One place where your tools, devices, software, connected environments, and workflows can finally work together.

Three connected tiers are planned: Infinity Zero, a native desktop AI workspace with a local-model option; Infinity, the wider companion for supported apps and devices; and Infinity², the longer-term immersive world and hardware vision. Each has its own delivery milestones.

Campaign preview: The Kickstarter project is not live yet. Rewards and delivery details are proposals until the live project page is published.

Why Infinity

Today, every device and service has its own interface, preferences, automations, assistants, and limits. Infinity is designed to sit above that fragmentation.

Infinity can synchronize your preferences across environments, adapt interfaces after load, connect compatible apps and services, automate repetitive work, expose capabilities through one coherent layer, and carry your personal configuration with you.

The goal is simple: technology should adapt to you—not the other way around.

What Infinity Does

These are planned capabilities, not all shipping today. Infinity Zero is the current native desktop prototype; Infinity and Infinity² are later tiers. The final project page must demonstrate the working prototype and disclose undeveloped integrations.

  • One Account. Everything Syncs. Preferences and supported settings travel with you.
  • Your AI Companion. One persistent AI layer that can route tasks to the right capability or model.
  • All Your Apps. One Home. Compatible tools and services in one coherent workspace.
  • Automate Anything. Repeated steps become reusable actions and workflows.
  • Privacy First. You decide access, retention, and where data lives.
  • Works Anywhere. Omniplatform, not a single-device app.
  • Open Platform. Adapters, extensions, automations, and integrations expand what Infinity can do.
  • Built to Evolve. New capabilities without forcing you to rebuild your digital life.

Risks & challenges

Infinity crosses operating systems, devices, services, AI providers, permissions, privacy boundaries, and hardware capabilities. Some integrations depend on third-party APIs, OS restrictions, hardware support, account permissions, and platform policies.

The campaign will publish milestones, distinguish prototypes from shipped functionality, disclose meaningful changes, and will not treat conceptual hardware as guaranteed finished production hardware until engineering and manufacturing support that claim.

AI is used in the product and in preparing parts of this campaign. The final project page must identify human work, distinguish generated concept art from working-product screenshots, and finalize compatibility, fulfillment costs, and delivery dates before pledges open.
